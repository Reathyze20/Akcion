Q2 Results – What Stood Out to Me

- D-BOX motion seats set an all-time quarterly sales record, up "more than 50%" YoY – management said "more than" twice, which matters for the math.

- My screen count estimate for Cinemark's D-BOX is up ~45–47% YoY in the same period, so a >50% revenue increase implies double-digit growth in revenue per screen (roughly in line with Cinemark's corporate average).

- Cinemark also posted record concession revenue (worldwide F&B > $400M for the first time), and they've already acknowledged that D-BOX patrons buy more concessions, which may be an underappreciated part of the story.

Screen Additions & Rollout Pace

- Cinemark added 112 D-BOX auditoriums over the last two quarters, which is >50 per quarter on average, even if this quarter came in closer to ~30 and the prior was closer to ~80.

- I'd prefer to see >50 net adds every quarter, but I don't read a single quarter in isolation. The key question is whether ROI on adding more D-BOX screens remains strong and Cinemark consistently reports that to be the case.

Bigger Picture & Thesis

- My base case: ~100 new D-BOX screens per quarter for the next several years (roughly 50 from Cinemark, 50 from other exhibitors).

- Net: I can't find anything I don't like here. Momentum is stable to improving, the content slate is strong, concessions are a hidden lever, and the long-term screen rollout story is intact.
