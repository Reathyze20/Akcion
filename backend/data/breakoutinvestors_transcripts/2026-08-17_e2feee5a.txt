Kuya Silver Production Targets and Earnings Discussion
Bryan led a discussion on Kuya Silver's recent earnings, addressing the company's ongoing goal of reaching 350 tons per day by year-end, with Ashleigh and Brad contributing questions and observations about management's guidance and operational progress.

Earnings and Production Guidance: Bryan explained that Kuya Silver's earnings were in line with expectations following their production report, noting that while the press release language might have raised concerns about the mill closing, the call clarified that there is no significant issue, just delays in achieving targets.

350 Tons Per Day Target: Ashleigh and Bryan discussed management's continued reference to the 350 tons per day goal by year-end, with Bryan stating his belief that there is zero chance of achieving this even for a single day, but noting that management did not explicitly refute the possibility, which he viewed as a positive sign.
