Kuya Mining Operational Update
Bryan provided a detailed update on Kuya Mining's production guidance, contractor changes, and mill acquisition progress, with Josh and other participants discussing expectations for production targets and financial results.

Production Guidance and Progress: Bryan explained that Kuya Mining's recent silver equivalent ounces guidance was consistent with expectations, noting improvements in June but expressing skepticism about reaching the 350 tons per day target in the near term. He emphasized that the company is taking a deliberate approach due to a strong balance sheet, allowing for careful contractor selection and compliance with legal requirements.

Contractor Changes and Exploration: Bryan reported that Kuya replaced its underground development contractor due to paperwork and insurance issues, but highlighted positive news that the company will operate three drills for exploration instead of two, which he views as a positive development.

Mill Acquisition Status: Bryan shared that the mill acquisition, expected to close soon, has faced delays but no significant negotiation issues. He relayed from discussions with management that the deal remains on track and is considered imminent.

Financial Results and Inventory: Bryan noted that Kuya's quarterly loss is expected to increase due to higher inventory levels at quarter end, explaining that more material was mined than delivered to the mill, which will impact reported costs and losses.

Management Guidance and Expectations: In response to Josh's question, Bryan clarified that management has effectively softened their tone on production targets through their latest guidance, indicating that the 350 tons per day goal is not immediately achievable.
